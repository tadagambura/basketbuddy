# BasketBuddy

**Shopping lists that remember**

> #31 of 33 in the build order · Harder · Revenue potential: Low–medium · Home & Household

## What it is

Reusable grocery lists with quantities, estimated prices and a running total, organised by store and aisle. Share a live list so whoever's at the shop sees what's needed.

## Why it's in the portfolio

Weekly use and a natural partner to PlateWeek and WorthIt. The category is crowded, so the edge is the running total and the link to meal plans.

## Who it's for

Households doing a weekly shop.

**The problem:** Rewriting the same list every week, and not knowing the total until the till.

## Version one

- Lists with quantities and estimated prices
- Reusable template lists
- Running estimated total
- Store-specific lists and aisle order
- Receive lists from PlateWeek

**Example:** A user loads their 'weekly shop' template, adds two items, and sees an estimated £54 before leaving home.

## Later

- Live shared lists between people (needs sync)
- Price history
- Unlimited lists
